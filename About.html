About Dominion Primary School

Nurturing Minds, Building Futures Since 1999

Our Journey

1999

Founded

Dominion Primary School was established with a vision to provide quality education to primary school students in a nurturing environment.

2005

Expansion

Added new classrooms and modern facilities to accommodate growing student population and enhance learning experience.

2012

Technology Integration

Introduced computer labs and digital learning platforms to revolutionize teaching and learning methodologies.

2020

Digital Transformation

Successfully transitioned to hybrid learning during pandemic, implementing virtual classrooms and online assessment systems.

2024

Innovation Excellence

Achieved recognition as one of the best primary schools with AI-powered learning and secure digital infrastructure.

Our Digital Innovation

🌐 AI-Powered Virtual Classroom

Real-time translation and interactive learning experiences that break down language barriers and enable inclusive education for all students.

🎥 Live Classroom Monitoring

AI-powered analytics that track engagement and provide real-time insights into classroom dynamics for better educational outcomes.

📝 Smart Assessment System

Computer-Based Testing (CBT) with both online and offline capabilities ensuring secure, efficient, and fair examinations.

🔒 Secure Child Pick-up

Advanced security system ensuring students are only released to authorized guardians with real-time verification.

📊 Smart Attendance Tracking

Automated attendance system using RFID and biometric technology for accurate, transparent, and efficient record-keeping.

🤖 AI-Powered Learning

Personalized learning paths powered by artificial intelligence adapting to each student's pace and learning style.
