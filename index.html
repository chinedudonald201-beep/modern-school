Welcome to Dominion Primary School

At Dominion Primary School, we are committed to providing a nurturing and inclusive environment where students can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally. Our dedicated faculty and staff work tirelessly to create a supportive community that fosters growth, creativity, and lifelong learning.

Learn More
Mission

To provide a nurturing and inclusive environment where students can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally, fostering growth, creativity, and lifelong learning.

Vision

To be a leading primary school that empowers students to become responsible global citizens, equipped with the knowledge, skills, and values to succeed in an ever-changing world.

Achievement

Our school has achieved excellence in academics, with students consistently ranking among the top in national examinations. We also pride ourselves on our extracurricular programs.

Discipline

We promote positive behavior through a supportive discipline policy that emphasizes guidance, respect, and personal responsibility. Our approach helps students develop self-discipline and good character.
